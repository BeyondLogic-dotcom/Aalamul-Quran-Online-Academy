About Us

A lifelong journey with the Book of Allah

Aalamul Quran Online Academy is committed to helping Muslims of all ages develop a strong connection with the Holy Quran. We believe that learning the Quran is a lifelong journey, and our goal is to make that journey easy, enjoyable, and rewarding.

Whether you are a beginner or want to improve your Quran reading, Tajweed, memorisation (Hifz), or Islamic knowledge, we are here to help you achieve your learning goals with confidence and excellence.

Our Mission

To provide quality online Quran education that enables students to read, understand, and memorise the Holy Quran with proper Tajweed while strengthening their faith and Islamic values.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and respected online Quran academies by offering excellent Quran education through qualified teachers, personalised learning, and modern online teaching methods.

Our Courses

From the first letter to a heart full of Quran

Eight carefully structured courses for children and adults, taught one-to-one at your own pace.

  1. ٠١ أ ب ت

    Noorani Qaida

    For Beginners

    Start from the very first letter: the Arabic alphabet, harakat and joining rules that build a strong reading foundation.

  2. ٠٢ تَجْوِيد

    Quran Reading with Tajweed

    Children & Adults

    Recite fluently and beautifully with correct Makharij, Sifaat and the rules of Tajweed.

  3. ٠٣ حِفْظ

    Quran Memorization (Hifz)

    Children & Adults

    A structured memorisation plan with daily lessons, regular revision and personal guidance from a Hafiz.

  4. ٠٤ تَفْسِير

    Basic Tafseer

    Understanding

    Understand the meanings and messages of the Quran and how they guide everyday life.

  5. ٠٥ فِقْه

    Basic Fiqh (Shariah Masail)

    Practical Knowledge

    Learn the essential rulings of purity, prayer, fasting and daily worship in a simple, practical way.

  6. ٠٦ إِسْلَام

    Islamic Studies

    Faith & Character

    Aqeedah, the Seerah of the Prophet ﷺ, stories of the Prophets and Islamic manners.

  7. ٠٧ صَلَاة

    Namaz & Daily Duas

    Daily Practice

    Learn to pray Salah correctly, step by step, and memorise the Masnoon duas for daily life.

  8. ٠٨ عَرَبِي

    Basic Arabic Language

    Language of the Quran

    Build vocabulary and basic grammar to begin understanding the language of the Quran.

Not sure where to start? Book a free trial and our teacher will assess the right level for you.
